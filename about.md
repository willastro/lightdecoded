# About Me

![Will Rocha](https://media.licdn.com/dms/image/D4E03AQHxz1H9JYkC2A/profile-displayphoto-shrink_800_800/0/1706441900123?e=2147483647&v=beta&t=4lG4x9o8SOJ0JxjliRZ3DEZBAY9D3Ywa35cDGfrdMSY)

Hello! I'm **Dr. Will Rocha**, a **spectral and imaging data scientist** with a background in **astrophysics** and **astrochemistry**. I hold a PhD and recently completed a research position at the **University of Leiden**, where I developed and applied advanced computational methods for analyzing spectral and imaging data.

---

## 👩‍🔬 Research and Expertise
- **Spectroscopy & Spectral Fitting:** Developer of *ENIIGMA*, an AI-based spectral fitting tool for automated astrochemical analysis.
- **Imaging & Computer Vision:** Applying machine learning and deep learning techniques for pattern recognition in both astrophysical and biomedical imaging.
- **Astrochemistry:** Investigating the molecular composition of interstellar ices, supporting laboratory experiments and space telescope data interpretation.

---

## 💡 Current Focus
I’m currently expanding my expertise into **data science applications beyond astronomy**, especially in **biomedical imaging** and **industrial optical systems** such as those used by **ASML**. My recent projects include MRI image analysis using deep learning and optimization of spectral data modeling techniques.
